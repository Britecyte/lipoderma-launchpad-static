About Lipoderma®

Lipoderma® is a like-for-like solution designed to replace fat with fat — restoring the healthy adipose tissue that supports a natural, youthful-looking appearance. It is especially helpful for patients experiencing fat loss related to weight reduction, including GLP-1–associated changes, while also addressing tissue thinning that occurs with aging.

This resource hub was created to provide clinicians with the information needed to use Lipoderma with confidence, including education, guidance, and practical tools.

The goal is simple: restore healthy fat where it matters most.
